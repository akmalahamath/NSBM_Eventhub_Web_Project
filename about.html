About NSBM Green University

Discover. Connect. Participate.

Welcome to NSBM Green University

NSBM Green University is a leading higher education institution in Sri Lanka, dedicated to developing future-ready graduates through quality education, innovation, research, and industry-focused learning.

Located in Homagama, Sri Lanka, the university provides a modern learning environment where students can develop their academic knowledge, practical skills, creativity, leadership abilities, and professional experience.

A Modern University for a Digital Generation

NSBM provides an environment designed to support modern university life. With contemporary facilities, technology-enabled learning, student communities, clubs, societies, and a wide range of academic and extracurricular activities, students have opportunities to learn both inside and outside the classroom.

University events play an important role in this experience. Academic sessions, workshops, competitions, sports activities, cultural events, career programmes, seminars, and student club activities allow students to connect, collaborate, and develop skills beyond their academic studies.

Digital Campus Experience

Why NSBM Event Hub?

NSBM Event Hub is designed to bring these university experiences together in one convenient digital platform. Instead of searching through different announcements, social media posts, and club communications, students can use Event Hub to discover upcoming events, explore categories, view event details, and register for activities from a centralized platform.
